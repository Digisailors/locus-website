## Key Findings Summary

### Navigine Platform Strengths
- **Multi-technology support**: BLE, Wi-Fi, UWB, and more
- **Comprehensive analytics**: Advanced reporting and visualization
- **Proven scalability**: 3000+ installations globally
- **Industry diversity**: Solutions for all 15 target industries
- **Case study validation**: Toyota, Halliburton, Kesko implementations

### Blueiot Platform Strengths
- **High precision**: Sub-meter (0.3m) accuracy with Bluetooth AoA
- **Gartner recognition**: Included in Magic Quadrant
- **Simplified ecosystem**: Complete hardware/software solution
- **Manufacturing focus**: Strong industrial applications
- **Healthcare precision**: Medical equipment and patient tracking

## Technology Selection Guide

### Choose Navigine if:
- You need multi-technology support
- Your application requires comprehensive analytics
- You operate across diverse environments
- Your focus is on customer/visitor experience
- You need proven scalability (3000+ installations)

### Choose Blueiot if:
- You prioritize sub-meter (0.3m) accuracy
- Your primary technology need is Bluetooth AoA
- You want simplified deployment with their ecosystem
- Your focus is manufacturing, healthcare, or logistics precision
- You prefer Gartner-recognized technology
